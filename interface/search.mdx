Press <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on Windows) anywhere in Proprietas to open universal search, or click the **Search** field at the top of the sidebar. It's the fastest way to move around without clicking through menus.

## What You Can Find

One search box, everything in it:

- **Sites**: jump straight to a site's page
- **Compliance obligations**: find a regime on a site and its status
- **Leases** and **deeds**: open a lease by tenant or address
- **Documents**: locate a certificate or a PDF you've uploaded
- **Issues**, **work orders**, **rent** and **assets**
- **Filtered views**: open a grid already filtered to what you typed
- **Pages**: go to any part of the app, including pages that aren't in the sidebar, such as Leases, Inbox and Audit Log
- **Chats**: reopen an [assistant](/interface/assistant) conversation

## Before You Type

With an empty search box, the palette shows:

- **Recent**: the items you opened most recently
- **Recent Searches**: what you searched for last
- **Quick Actions**: **New Issue**, **New Work Order**, **Add Site**, **Add Contractor**, **Open Inbox** and **Open Settings**

**New Issue** and **New Work Order** open the matching list and put your cursor in its new-record row, ready to type a title. See [Logging an Issue Yourself](/facilities/issues#logging-an-issue-yourself).

## Type Tabs

Results group by type as you type. Tabs across the top of the palette show **All** plus each type that has results. Press <kbd>←</kbd> or <kbd>→</kbd> to switch tab. While you're typing, the arrows only switch tab when the cursor is at the start or end of your search.

## Keys

A footer at the bottom of the palette lists the keys:

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Close |
| <kbd>←</kbd> / <kbd>→</kbd> | Change type tab |
| <kbd>↑</kbd> / <kbd>↓</kbd> | Move through results |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Open the selected result |

Search respects your active [site scope](/interface/navigating#site-scope), so when you're focused on one site, its results come first.

<Note>
  Documents marked as **[sensitive](/security/overview#sensitive-documents)** don't appear in Cmd K results for anyone but an organisation admin. Their titles never surface in search to other members. Sensitive documents are visible only to admins everywhere they appear, search included.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Treat Cmd K as your default way to navigate. It's almost always faster than the sidebar once a workspace has more than a handful of sites.
</Tip>

## Search vs the Assistant

Search **takes you to** something that exists: a page, a record, an action. The [assistant](/interface/assistant) **answers questions** about your portfolio ("which sites have an overdue EICR?"). Reach for search when you know what you're looking for. Reach for the assistant when you have a question.
