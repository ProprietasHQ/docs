Proprietas has one consistent shell across every authenticated page: a sidebar on the left, a header across the top of each page, and the page canvas in the middle. Once you know those three, you know where everything is.

## The Sidebar

From top to bottom, the sidebar holds:

1. **The workspace menu**, showing the site you're scoped to (or **All Sites**).
2. **A search field.** Click it to open [universal search](/interface/search) (<kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>).
3. **Home, Chat and Inbox.** Home shows the page list below. Chat lists your [assistant chats](/interface/assistant#managing-chats). Inbox shows your recent [notifications and documents to review](/interface/inbox).
4. **The page list**, grouped the way estates teams work:

| Group | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| **Top** | Dashboard |
| **Operations** | Files, Calendar, Assets, Issues, Work Orders |
| **Estate** | Sites (with [floor plans](/facilities/floor-plans) per site), Compliance, Rent, Contractors |
| **Bottom** | Settings |

5. **Your account menu**, at the very bottom. See [Your Account Menu](#your-account-menu).

Entries show a **count badge** when something needs attention: overdue compliance, open issues, open work orders, overdue rent, pending join requests. A badge only appears when there's something to act on.

You can collapse groups and reorder items for your own workspace. Settings stays pinned.

## Sites and Units

A **Site** is a building or location you're responsible for. A **Unit** is a leasable space inside a multi-unit site, such as a floor or a shop. The sidebar entry is **Sites**. See [Sites & scope](/getting-started/properties-and-scope).

## Site Scope

Proprietas is portfolio-wide by default. Pick a site from the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar, or from the first item in the page header, and the whole app narrows to it: every list, every status, every report. Your choice persists across reloads, so you can work inside one site for as long as you need, then choose **All Sites** to go back to the whole portfolio.

The page header shows the site's name while a scope is active.

<Note>
  When a number surprises you, check the scope first. You may be looking at one site rather than the whole portfolio, or the other way round.
</Note>

## Your Account Menu

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar to open the account menu:

- **Settings**
- **Members**
- **Billing**
- **Give Feedback**
- **Get Help**, which opens these docs
- **Sign Out**

Next to your name, a button links to the [desktop app](/interface/desktop-app) download. It's hidden when you're already in the desktop app.

## The Inbox

Notifications and documents waiting for review share one [Inbox](/interface/inbox). Open it from the Inbox icon in the sidebar.

## Calendar

**Calendar** (under Operations) is the in-app month view for compliance deadlines, lease breaks and reviews, rent, and scheduled work, colour-coded by overdue, due soon and upcoming. To mirror those dates into Apple, Google or Outlook, see [Calendar sync](/interface/calendar-sync).

## Lists and Data Grids

The high-volume surfaces, [Issues](/facilities/issues), [Work Orders](/facilities/work-orders) and [Assets](/facilities/assets), are **spreadsheet-style data grids**. They share one toolbar (filter, sort, search and saved views), keep their header and toolbar frozen while the rows scroll, and support inline edit and keyboard navigation (see [Keyboard shortcuts](/interface/keyboard-shortcuts#data-grids)). Universal search and the assistant can drop you straight into one of these grids **pre-filtered** to the rows you asked for.

The Issues and Work Orders grids end with a **+ New Issue** or **+ New Work Order** row, so you can add a record without leaving the list. See [Logging an Issue Yourself](/facilities/issues#logging-an-issue-yourself).

## The Side Panel

Click a row and the record opens in a **side panel** beside the list, so the list stays in view. The panel's header shows the record's name and type, with two buttons:

| Button | Key | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Expand** | <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>O</kbd> | Opens the record as a full page |
| **Close** | <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Closes the panel |

## Settings

Everything administrative lives under **Settings**: your account, organisation profile, members and invitations, single sign-on and verified domains, billing and AI usage, calendar subscriptions, MCP connectors, and notification preferences. Admin-only sections are clearly marked and hidden from non-admins. Settings also links to the [Audit Log](/security/audit-trail#in-the-app).

Settings opens as a panel over the dashboard. Each panel has its own link, so you can bookmark it or send it to a colleague: `/settings/<panel>` opens that panel. For example:

| Link | Opens |
| --- | --- |
| `/settings/members` | Members |
| `/settings/billing` | Billing |
| `/settings/notifications` | Notifications |
| `/settings/security` | Security |

An unrecognised panel name opens **Your Account**.
