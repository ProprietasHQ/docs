Compliance is the spine of Proprietas. Every site is measured against the UK statutory regimes that apply to it, and the system tells you exactly what's overdue, due soon and valid — with reminders that arrive before a deadline does.

## Regimes and Obligations

When you create a workspace, Proprietas seeds the full catalog of UK statutory regimes — Fire Risk Assessment, legionella/L8, EICR, gas safety, LOLER, asbestos, PAT, EPC, F-Gas, TM44, fire alarm, emergency lighting, insurance certificates and more.

## Site Purpose

Each site's **Purpose** decides which items it tracks. Set it from the site's page under **Purpose**, and Proprietas adds the items UK law and industry guidance expect for that kind of building.

Every workplace purpose starts from the same core: fire risk assessment, weekly fire alarm tests and annual servicing, monthly and annual emergency lighting, extinguisher servicing, legionella risk assessment, monthly water temperatures, EICR, PAT and the asbestos management plan. Each purpose then adds its own:

| Purpose | Adds to the core |
| --- | --- |
| **Office, Commercial, Mixed-use** | Employers' liability insurance, boiler service, EPC |
| **Retail, Industrial** | Employers' liability insurance, boiler service, powered door and gate service, EPC |
| **Warehouse** | Employers' liability insurance, powered door and gate service, racking inspection, EPC |
| **Educational** | Boiler service, Display Energy Certificate, PE and gym equipment, cold water tank inspection |
| **Hotel** | Employers' liability insurance, gas safety, outlet flushing, showerhead descaling, cold water tank inspection, catering gas, kitchen extract clean, EPC |
| **Healthcare** | Employers' liability insurance, boiler service, TMV servicing, outlet flushing, showerhead descaling, EPC |
| **Care home** | Employers' liability insurance, gas safety, hoist and sling examination every 6 months, TMV servicing, outlet flushing, showerhead descaling, window restrictor and bed rail checks, EPC |

Homes don't use the workplace core:

| Purpose | Tracks |
| --- | --- |
| **Residential** | Landlord gas safety record, EICR, EPC, legionella risk assessment, smoke and CO alarm check |
| **HMO** | HMO licence, fire risk assessment, the residential items, monthly smoke alarm tests, asbestos management plan |

Each item keeps its statutory interval: a landlord gas safety record every year, an EICR every 5 years (3 for industrial sites), a care-home hoist every 6 months. Where the law sets the interval by risk assessment, as with a fire risk assessment, the default is annual and you can change it.

Changing a site's purpose adds the new items and retires the ones that no longer apply. Retired items keep their certificates and history.

For rented homes, the smoke and CO alarm check falls due on the start date of each new tenancy, as the 2022 regulations require.

### Add a Single Item

Some items depend on what's fitted rather than the building type: lifts, air conditioning, sprinklers, medical gas pipelines, specialised ventilation, Pseudomonas sampling. Open the site's compliance list, press **+** and pick from the full catalogue. Items marked **Add if fitted** are the ones usually found in that kind of building, listed first.

Uploading a certificate for an item the site doesn't track yet also adds it.

## Statuses

Each **site × item** produces a **compliance obligation** with a default frequency and a computed next-due date. Obligations show one of these statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Valid** | A current certificate is on file |
| **Due soon** | Due within the next 30 days |
| **Overdue** | Past the due date with no fresh certificate |
| **No certificate** | Tracked, but no certificate has been uploaded yet. Shown with a dashed outline |
| **Not due** | Tracked, with nothing due yet |

## The Compliance View

The compliance page is a **portfolio grid**: one row per site and one column per category (Fire, Water, Electrical, Gas, Lifts and so on). Each cell shows the most urgent status in that category, so the whole estate's position reads at a glance. Scroll sideways for more categories.

Filter by **Status** to find every site with an overdue item, or filter a single category column. Search, sort and column choices work as on every other grid, and the grid is **pre-filterable by deep link**, so search and the [assistant](/interface/assistant) can drop you onto the overdue slice they're describing.

Click a site to open its items in a [side panel](/interface/navigating#the-side-panel), grouped by category. Choose **Open site compliance** for the full list.

## Certificates

Upload a certificate against an obligation — or just drag the PDF onto the page and let intake route it. Proprietas reads the document, confirms the type, inspector and expiry, and closes the obligation with the new date.

<Note>
  Statutory dates the parser isn't confident about are held for a human to confirm rather than auto-filed — so a low-confidence read never silently sets the wrong expiry.
</Note>

## Reminders

Every obligation runs a pre-expiry workflow:

- **Flags at 90, 60, 30 and 7 days** before expiry, by email and in-app.
- At the **7-day** threshold, an automatic **work order** can be raised and routed to the right contractor with the existing certificate attached.
- When the contractor uploads the new certificate through their portal, the obligation closes itself with the fresh expiry.

You control which reminders reach you in **Settings → Notifications**.

## Responsible Person

Every site has a named **Responsible Person** from day one, reassignable in a click. Reminders and escalations route to them, not a shared inbox.

## Audit Pack

One click produces an **audit pack PDF** — the certificates, dates and history an inspector (Ofsted, CQC, DSPT, insurer) wants to see, ready to hand over.
