Everything in Proprietas hangs off a [site](#sites-and-units). Compliance obligations, leases, issues, work orders, documents and rent all attach to a site (or to a [unit](#sites-and-units) within one). Get your sites right and the rest of the app organises itself.

## Sites and Units

A **Site** is a building or location you're responsible for. You'll find them all under **Sites** in the sidebar. Sites come in two shapes:

- **Standalone**: the site is the unit. A single school, office or shop. Every record attaches directly to it.
- **Multi-Unit**: the site holds **Units**, the leasable spaces inside it, such as floors, shops or blocks. Exchange Tower's twelve floors are one site with twelve units. Records can attach at the building level (a whole-building FRA) or the unit level (a single tenant's lease).

Each site carries a **type** (educational, office, care, healthcare, hotel, retail, residential, commercial) and a **tenure** (owned, leased, licensed, managed). The type drives which compliance regimes get seeded. See [Compliance](/compliance/overview).

Sites also hold [floor plans](/facilities/floor-plans). Drop a PDF or DWG, pin assets to rooms, and walk the building in 2D or 3D.

## Adding Sites

<Steps>
  <Step title="Let a document create it">
    The quickest path: **drop a commercial lease** (or a whole folder of them). Proprietas reads the demised premises, derives the building address, and **creates the site automatically**, grouping a multi-floor building's leases into one site with a unit per floor. Certificates do the same: drop a gas cert for a building you haven't added yet and the site is created to receive it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add one at a time">
    Use **Add Site** on the Sites page, or from [Cmd K](/interface/search). Enter the address. Proprietas canonicalises it against the national address database so two spellings of the same building don't become two sites.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bulk-add a portfolio">
    Paste a list of addresses into **Bulk Add**. Each line is verified, de-duplicated by postcode, and multi-floor buildings are auto-grouped into one site with units. Stand up a portfolio in minutes, not days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Responsible Person">
    Every site has a named [Responsible Person](/compliance/reminders#routing-to-the-right-people) from day one: the individual reminders escalate to. Reassign it in a click.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Adding Units

On a multi-unit site, open **Units** and choose **Add Unit**, or drop a lease and Proprietas creates the unit for you. A standalone site with several leasable spaces can be split with **Convert to Multi-Unit**.

## The Active Site Scope

The most important control in the app is the [site scope](/interface/navigating#site-scope). Proprietas is portfolio-wide by default, but pick a site from the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar, or open one of its units, and the **entire app narrows to it**: dashboard, compliance, leases, rent, issues, work orders, documents, even the audit pack and public reporting link.

<Note>
  Your scope is remembered across reloads. When a number surprises you, check the scope first. You may be looking at one unit rather than the whole estate.
</Note>

The page header shows the site's name whenever a scope is active, so you always know what you're looking at. Choose **All Sites** to clear it.
