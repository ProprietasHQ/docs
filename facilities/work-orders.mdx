A work order is a unit of work assigned to a contractor: a repair, a service, a compliance renewal. Proprietas tracks it from raised to paid.

## Where Work Orders Come From

- **Raised by hand** from the Work Orders list, for any task (see below)
- **Promoted from an [issue](/facilities/issues)** when a reported problem needs work
- **Raised automatically** at the 7-day compliance threshold to renew a lapsing certificate (see [Reminders](/compliance/reminders))
- **Raised on cadence** by a [PPM schedule](/compliance/planned-maintenance)

## Raising a Work Order by Hand

Editors can raise a work order straight from the list, without an issue or a compliance finding behind it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new row">
    Click **+ New Work Order** at the bottom of the Work Orders grid. You can also choose **New Work Order** from the quick actions in [Cmd K](/interface/search#before-you-type), which takes you straight to that row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type a title">
    Type what needs doing and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a site">
    Choose the site. If you're scoped to one site, it's already filled in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The work order saves as soon as it has a title and a site, and opens in the [side panel](/interface/navigating#the-side-panel) beside the list so you can add the rest. If your current filters would hide the new work order, a message tells you and offers **Open**.

## Numbers and Statuses

Each carries a number (WO-0001), title, priority (low / medium / high / **emergency**) and status: **Open → Scheduled → In progress → On hold → Completed / Cancelled**.

<Note>
  The work-order list is a **spreadsheet-style data grid**, shared with [Issues](/facilities/issues) and [Assets](/facilities/assets). Filter, sort and search from a fixed toolbar, save views, and the header stays frozen while the rows scroll. Search and the [assistant](/interface/assistant) can open it pre-filtered via **Open in grid**.
</Note>

## Competence Gating

When you assign a contractor, Proprietas checks their competence certificates (Gas Safe, NICEIC, SafeContractor, CHAS). If one has expired, the assignment can be **blocked until it's refreshed**, so an out-of-ticket contractor never ends up on a gas job. See [Contractors](/facilities/contractors).

## Tendering and Quotes

For organisations that need competitive quotes, as charity trustees and MATs often do, a work order can require a **minimum number of quotes** before it's accepted. Contractors submit quotes; you accept one. Accepting below the quote threshold requires a written override reason, which is recorded.

Multiple quotes live on the work order, with one accepted at a time; a superseded quote stays in the history.

## Payments

Log payments against a work order (deposit, milestone, final), each with amount, method, reference and an optional invoice. Payments can be voided without losing the audit history, so the financial trail is always intact.

## Conversation

Every work order carries a **threaded conversation** between your team and the assigned contractor. Comments appear in the work order's side panel and full page on your side, and in the [contractor portal](/facilities/contractor-portal) on theirs, so "when can you attend?" and the answer live on the job itself instead of in someone's inbox. New replies arrive in [real time](/interface/inbox#live-in-the-app).

## Closing the Loop

When work is done, the contractor marks it complete, often through the [portal](/facilities/contractor-portal), uploads any certificate or report, and the linked issue or obligation updates. Every status change and comment is logged.
