Issues are how problems get into Proprietas: a broken boiler, a fire door propped open, a leak. Anyone can raise one, and your team triages and acts.

## Reporting Without an Account

Every site and unit has a **public reporting link** (and QR code). A tenant, member of staff or visitor opens it, describes the problem, and it lands in your Issues list with **no signup and no login**. They get a token to revisit and check the status without an account.

This is deliberate: the people who spot problems are rarely the people with logins. Removing the account barrier is what keeps your issue data real.

## Logging an Issue Yourself

Editors can also log an issue by hand, for example after a phone call or a site walk.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new row">
    Click **+ New Issue** at the bottom of the Issues grid. You can also choose **New Issue** from the quick actions in [Cmd K](/interface/search#before-you-type), which takes you straight to that row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type a title">
    Type what's wrong and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a site">
    Choose the site it belongs to. If you're scoped to one site, it's already filled in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The issue saves as soon as it has a title and a site, and opens in the [side panel](/interface/navigating#the-side-panel) so you can add the rest. If your current filters would hide the new issue, a message tells you and offers **Open**.

## Automatic Triage

When an issue arrives, Proprietas reads the description and proposes a **category** and **priority** (low / medium / high / urgent), with a short triage note. You can override either, and every correction you make is captured, so the triage gets better over time.

## Working an Issue

Issues move through a clear lifecycle: **New → Triaged → Assigned → In progress → Resolved → Closed** (with a Duplicate path). The list works like an inbox: unread issues are marked, filters cover status, priority, assignee ("mine" / "unassigned") and text search, and the default view shows what's still open.

Click an issue to open it in the side panel beside the list. Assign it to a team member, leave notes on the thread, and when it needs real repair work, **promote it to a [work order](/facilities/work-orders)**. The link between the issue and the work order is kept, so the original report stays connected to the fix.

<Note>
  Issues, [Work Orders](/facilities/work-orders) and [Assets](/facilities/assets) all use the same **spreadsheet-style data grid**. Filter, sort and search from a fixed toolbar, save views, and the header stays frozen while the rows scroll. Search results and the [assistant](/interface/assistant) can drop you straight into a pre-filtered grid via **Open in grid**.
</Note>

<Note>
  Issues sitting too long with one owner are flagged automatically, with priority-aware thresholds, so nothing quietly rots in someone's queue.
</Note>
